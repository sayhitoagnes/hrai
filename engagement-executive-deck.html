Loukroi engagement survey05 / 07

Leadership decision

Use the next operating cycle to turn sentiment into sustainable performance.

Establish the resource baseline

Ask each function to quantify capacity, tooling, budget, and approval constraints against committed work. Make trade-offs visible at the leadership table.

Reduce delivery friction

Select the most repeated cross-team handoffs and remove one or two major bottlenecks. Pair this with clear ownership and decision rights.

Close the loop visibly

Share the findings, the choices made, and accountable owners. Run a short pulse after implementation to test whether employees experience a practical change.

Loukroi engagement survey06 / 07

90-day action frame

Focus on evidence, targeted interventions, and proof that the work got easier.

0–30 DAYS

Diagnose

Run structured listening sessions on resource constraints; map demand, capacity, and the critical work that is being delayed.

31–60 DAYS

Act

Fund, stop, simplify, or sequence work based on the baseline. Prioritize shared pain points over isolated fixes.

61–90 DAYS

Validate

Pulse the four team-health items and communicate the before-and-after decisions to every employee.

Success measure: lift team resources first, while holding pride and intent to stay at their current levels.
