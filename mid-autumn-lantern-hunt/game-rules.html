月餅謎局 · 燈謎The Mooncake Puzzle · How to take part

The Mooncake Puzzle月餅謎局 · 燈謎 · How to take part

這是一項約十分鐘、自願參加的網上中秋小遊戲。歡迎 3 至 5 位同事組隊，一起配月餅、擺團圓桌、猜燈謎。分數最高（同分則較快完成）的隊伍可獲 $500 現金券。

This is a voluntary online Mid-Autumn game of about ten minutes. Form a team of 3 to 5 colleagues, match mooncakes, set a reunion table, and solve lantern riddles. The team with the highest score — or, if tied, the faster finish — wins a $500 cash coupon.

活動目的Purpose

中秋講求團圓。未能同桌時，我們仍可以一小隊同事、十來分鐘，一起過節：配月餅、擺團圓桌、猜燈謎，為未能同聚的同事題一句話。

Mid-Autumn is a festival of reunion. When we cannot sit at one table, a small team and ten minutes are still enough to celebrate: matching mooncakes, setting a reunion table, answering lantern riddles, and writing one line for colleagues who are not in the same room.

參加步驟How to join

  1. 組隊並報名（9 月 22 日或之前） Form a team and register (by 22 September) 每隊 3 至 5 人，推選一位隊長。請隊長把隊員名單及隊長姓名電郵至 Agnes（agnwong@hkac.org.hk）。 Teams of 3 to 5, with one representative. The representative emails the member list and their name to Agnes (agnwong@hkac.org.hk).
  2. 收取遊戲連結（9 月 23 日） Receive the game link (23 September) 連結只發送給隊長。無須登入。可用頁面右上角切換「繁／EN」。請與隊員一起完成。 The link is sent to the team representative only. No login is required. Use 繁 / EN at the top right. Please play together.
  3. 提交分數截圖（9 月 24 日） Submit a score screenshot (24 September) 把遊戲最後一頁（結語卡）截圖，由隊長電郵交回 Agnes。每隊只可提交一次，並只接受電郵。 Screenshot the closing card and email it to Agnes. One submission per team, by email only.

四個關卡怎麼玩How each stage works

  1. 序：點一盞燈 Prologue: light one lantern 輕觸畫面中的花燈，待燈亮後按「到謎局」。 Tap the lantern on screen. When it is lit, continue to the first game.
  2. 第一局：八對月餅（記憶配對） Stage 1: eight mooncake pairs (memory match) 棋盤有 16 枚月餅，共八對。先點一枚，再點另一枚。成對則留在面上；不成對則翻回。找齊八對後，進入團圓桌。翻次會記錄在結語卡，但不計入分數。 There are 16 cakes and eight pairs. Tap one cake, then another. A match stays face up; a miss turns back. Find all eight pairs to continue. Your number of turns is shown on the closing card and does not affect the score.
  3. 第二局：團圓桌 Stage 2: the reunion table 畫面出現十二件節慶物品，只有四件屬於中秋團圓桌。請先選滿當前空位的件數（開始時為四件），再按「請入席」。正確的會留在桌上；不屬於中秋的會被撤下。請補上空位，直至四席坐滿。活動不會在確認前透露哪些物品正確。 Twelve festival items appear. Only four belong at a Mid-Autumn table. Select as many items as there are empty seats (four at the start), then tap Seat them. Correct items stay; the others are cleared. Fill the remaining seats until four are seated. The activity does not reveal the correct items until you confirm a set.
  4. 第三局：六題燈謎 Stage 3: six lantern riddles 每題四選一，另有提示。選錯仍可繼續，該題不得分。題目涵蓋詩詞、成語燈謎及香港掌故。 Each question has four choices and a hint. A miss still continues; that question scores zero. Topics include verse, idiom riddles, and Hong Kong folklore.
  5. 題燈：寫一句 Inscription: write one line 為未能同室的同事寫一句，最多 40 字。可用畫面提供的成句，也可自撰。此句會出現在結語卡，不計分。 Write one line for colleagues who cannot gather tonight, up to 40 characters. You may use a prompt on screen or your own words. The line appears on your closing card and is not scored.

Maximum score: 180. Your score appears on the closing card. Competing teams should have the representative email a screenshot of that page to Human Resources.

請注意Please note

這是自願活動。分數最高的隊伍獲 $500 現金券；同分則以完成時間較短者勝出。每隊只可提交一次，並只接受電郵。遊戲本身不會儲存紀錄；請截圖保留分數。建議一次完成，重新整理頁面須從頭開始。

This activity is voluntary. The highest-scoring team wins a $500 cash coupon; a tied score is decided by the shorter finish time. One email submission per team. The game page does not store a record, so please screenshot your score. Finish in one sitting; refreshing starts again.
