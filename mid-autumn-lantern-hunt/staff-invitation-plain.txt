4. 回答六題燈謎（詩詞、成語及香港掌故）。
5. 為未能同聚的同事題寫一句（最多 40 字）。
6. 收取個人結語卡。滿分 180 分。

Dear colleagues,

Mid-Autumn Festival is approaching. As we cannot gather at one table, Human Resources invites all staff to a short online activity, The Mooncake Puzzle / 月餅謎局 · 燈謎. It is a quiet way to share the festival: matching mooncakes, setting a reunion table, and answering lantern riddles from wherever you are.

The activity takes about 10 minutes in a desktop or phone browser. No login is required.

What to expect
1. Light one lantern.
2. Match eight pairs of mooncakes (memory game).
3. From twelve festival items, seat the four that belong at a Mid-Autumn table.
4. Answer six lantern riddles (verse, idioms, and Hong Kong folklore).
5. Write one line (up to 40 characters) for colleagues who cannot gather tonight.
6. Receive a personal closing card. Maximum score: 180.

How to take part
1. Open the activity: https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/sayhitoagnes/hrai/blob/cursor/hkac-mid-autumn-lantern-hunt-1baf/mid-autumn-lantern-hunt/index.html
2. Enter your name (it appears only on your closing card and is not collected).
3. Follow the on-screen instructions. Please finish in one sitting; refreshing the page starts again.

Suggested window: 18–25 September 2026 (Mid-Autumn Festival). Please join at a convenient time.

Please note
- This is a goodwill activity only. There is no lucky draw, prize, or published ranking. Your score appears only on your closing card.
- Your answers stay on your device. The page does not send email and does not store personal data.
- Full playing rules and scoring are in the attached rules document.

Please contact Human Resources with any questions. We wish you a peaceful Mid-Autumn.
