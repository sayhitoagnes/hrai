You’re invited: Mid-Autumn team game · 誠邀組隊｜月餅謎局 · 燈謎

Hong Kong Arts Centre · 香港藝術中心

一起過中秋，歡迎組隊來玩
A short team game for Mid-Autumn

月餅謎局 · 燈謎  |  The Mooncake Puzzle  |  約 10 分鐘 · 自願參加

各位同事：

中秋快樂！雖然今年未能圍坐一桌，我們仍很想與你一起過節。

人事部誠邀大家參加自願網上小遊戲「月餅謎局 · 燈謎」。只需約 10 分鐘，用電腦或手機瀏覽器即可，無須登入。歡迎找身邊同事組成一隊，一起配月餅、擺團圓桌、猜燈謎，為這個中秋添一點歡笑。

如何參加

  1. 組成 3 至 5 人一隊，並推選一位隊長。
  2. 請隊長於 2026 年 9 月 22 日或之前，把隊員名單及隊長姓名電郵至人事部 Agnes（agnwong@hkac.org.hk）。
  3. 9 月 23 日，遊戲連結會發送給各隊隊長。請由隊長開啟遊戲，與隊員一起完成。
  4. 請於 9 月 24 日，把遊戲最後一頁（結語卡）的分數截圖，由隊長電郵交回 Agnes。每隊只可提交一次，並只接受電郵提交。

報名組隊 Email your team list

獎項

分數最高的隊伍將獲 $500 現金券。如多於一隊同得最高分，以完成時間較短的隊伍勝出。

這是自願活動。無論是否參賽，都祝你中秋愉快。

如有查詢，歡迎聯絡人事部或 Agnes。

祝中秋快樂。

人事部
香港藝術中心
灣仔港灣道 2 號

Dear colleagues,

Happy Mid-Autumn! We may not sit at the same table this year, and we would still love to celebrate with you.

Human Resources invites you to a voluntary online game, The Mooncake Puzzle / 月餅謎局 · 燈謎. It takes about 10 minutes on a computer or phone browser, with no login. Form a small team with colleagues, match mooncakes, set a reunion table, and solve lantern riddles — a short, festive break together.

How to take part

  1. Form a team of 3 to 5 colleagues and choose one team representative.
  2. By 22 September 2026, the representative emails the member list and the representative’s name to Agnes in Human Resources (agnwong@hkac.org.hk).
  3. On 23 September, the game link will be sent to each team representative. Please open it together and complete the game as a team.
  4. On 24 September, the representative emails a screenshot of the final score page (the closing card) to Agnes. Each team may submit only once, and only by email.

Prize

The team with the highest score wins a $500 cash coupon. If more than one team shares the highest score, the team that finished in the shorter time wins.

This activity is voluntary. Whether you join the game or not, we wish you a joyful Mid-Autumn.

Please contact Human Resources or Agnes with any questions.

Warm regards,
Human Resources
Hong Kong Arts Centre
2 Harbour Road, Wan Chai
