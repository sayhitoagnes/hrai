Run the celebration電郵即可完成的中秋活動

  1. Save this file to a shared drive (OneDrive, SharePoint, Google Drive, or the staff intranet) and copy the sharing link. Anyone with the link can play — no login.
  2. Open Setup, enter the HR mailbox that will collect tickets, the prize, and the deadline.
  3. Copy the invitation email and send it to all staff (~70 people). They play on a phone or computer, then click “Email my lucky ticket”.
  4. After the deadline, search your inbox for the subject line [HKAC Mid-Autumn]. Paste those names into Lucky draw and pick one winner.
  5. If you cannot share a link, use the plain-text riddle in the invitation email. Staff reply with answers; everyone who replies still enters the draw.

Fair play: every completed hunt (or email reply) has an equal chance. Score is only for the blessing card.
