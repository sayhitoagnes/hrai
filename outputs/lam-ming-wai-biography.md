# Brief Biography — Lam Ming Wai (Nicola)

**Word count:** 198

Lam Ming Wai (Nicola) is a Fine Arts graduate of The Chinese University of Hong Kong and an admission scholarship recipient, with experience in arts administration, exhibitions, community programming and art education.

At the Hong Kong Arts Centre (2024–2025), she designed social media materials, supported RE: Tai Kok Tsui Co-creation Space, and assisted programmes. She also served as on-site assistant and invigilator for Sullivan + Strumpf at Art Basel Hong Kong 2024, and interned at the Social Welfare Department, compiling feedback from over 50 participants and producing illustrations and layout for a psychology publication.

In Taiwan she interned at National Taiwan Normal University, supporting Professor Koon Wai Bong’s solo exhibition with Asia Art Center, and later joined TAIWANscape as curator-exhibitor. She currently teaches drawing at Cheuk’s Art Gallery.

On campus she chaired The Art of CUHK Design Team (2023–2024), served as Vice-President of the 2025 PR Team, and organised student exhibitions Call It a DAY and PeekaBoo. She enjoys studio practice, exhibition-making, graphic design and working with young people. Fluent in Cantonese, Mandarin and English, with intermediate Japanese, she looks forward to contributing to the Hong Kong Arts Centre’s mission of making contemporary art accessible to diverse audiences.
