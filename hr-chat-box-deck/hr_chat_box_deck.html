HR Chat box product brief 01 / 07

01 · App idea

A knowledge-base chatbot that turns policy documents into instant answers

Employees ask about leave, benefits, and workplace guidelines. The chatbot responds in real time from approved HR content — reducing wait time and repetitive inbox load.

What it is

An HR Knowledge Base Chatbot designed to answer employee questions on company policies, benefits, and workplace guidelines in real time.

Why it matters

Office staff get 24/7 clarity on day-to-day HR topics. HR keeps one trusted source of truth and spends less time restating standard answers.

HR Chat box product brief 02 / 07

02 · Main users

Scoped to office employees and HR operations

Frontline staff are excluded to keep the MVP focused on office policy, benefits, and guideline queries.

Primary · Employee / Staff

Ask and receive

  • Asks about benefits, policies, and onboarding
  • Receives instant, 24/7 answers from the knowledge base
  • Uses Quick FAQ shortcuts for common topics (e.g. leave, bad weather, staff benefits)
Secondary · HR / HR Operations

Govern and support

  • Updates and manages the knowledge base
  • Answers escalated or unresolved questions
  • Tracks frequently asked topics to improve content

Out of scope for this build: frontline / field workforce channels

HR Chat box product brief 03 / 07

03 · Input information

Four content sources power every trusted reply

HR Policies & Staff Manual

Leave policies, code of conduct, remote work guidelines, and office protocols.

Benefits & Perks

Health insurance options, retirement plans, wellness stipends, and expense reimbursement steps.

FAQ Database

Common staff inquiries with pre-approved standard answers for consistent guidance.

AI Credit Data

Staff monthly AI credit quotas and usage logs to govern fair chatbot usage.

Design principle: answers come only from approved HR materials — not open web search — so tone and policy stay controlled.
HR Chat box product brief 04 / 07

04 · Expected functions

Four capabilities define the MVP promise

  1. 01
    Instant policy answers

    Provide accurate replies to office staff on company policies, benefits, and workplace guidelines.

  2. 02
    Contact HR escalation

    Route complex or unresolved questions to HR for manual review when the bot cannot answer confidently.

  3. 03
    Monthly AI credit limits

    Enforce a per-user monthly credit quota and notify staff when remaining credits are low.

  4. 04
    Knowledge base management

    Allow HR admins to upload, edit, and manage policy documents and FAQ content.

HR Chat box product brief 05 / 07

05 · User flow

Stormy morning: Signal No. 8, credits checked, answer delivered

STEP 01

Open chatbot

Employee opens HR Chat box on a stormy morning to check work arrangements.

STEP 02

Verify credits

System confirms the employee still has monthly AI credits before processing.

STEP 03

Ask the question

“Do I need to report to the office or work from home today?”

STEP 04

Policy reply

Reads latest bad-weather policy; replies to work remotely under Signal No. 8; deducts 1 credit.

STEP 05

Escalate if needed

If unanswered, the app shares Contact HR options for manual follow-up.

Outcome: the employee gets a clear, policy-backed answer in seconds — without waiting for an HR reply — while usage stays within the monthly credit budget.
